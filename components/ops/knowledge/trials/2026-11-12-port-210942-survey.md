Game build 210942 (the update of 2026-11-12) read against 201602's stock, scenario by scenario, before any play on it:
- **Layout.** Every scenario pack's Definition moved to `NDF/Scenarios/<scenario>/<Kind>.txn` with a `.txn.tag` and a
  `.dbg` ([[scenario-definition-layout]]); the Details are unchanged (79 Details packs of revision 210942 still ship
  `.ndfbin`). Trophy became TrophyDef.
- **Shapes.** Survivors' GDScript holds two more objects, an unreferenced `$/GDScript/SplashSoundDescriptor` and its
  stream at #108, so 201602's object n is n+2 from there on; its root is `TGDDescriptorLaunchEffetMap` (30 of 30 stock
  roots); its tags bind by `GameDesignAddonId` (2,133 of 2,133); `BonusScoreMission` is gone (105 stock uses on 201602,
  0 now) ([[game-build-classes]]).
- **Effects.** The 75 stock effect launches are the same three self-contained effects; GameFx now names its objects
  `_NdfIdentifierName` and ships in Transactions.dat ([[script-effects-self-contained]], [[scripted-nuke-effect]]).
- **Load rules.** In the 210942 rules table, `TGDDescriptorChangeStress` no longer tests `DamageType`
  ([[changestress-damagetype-zero]]), and `TGDDescriptorGereObjectif` gained a check that Position and
  UnitGroupPositionList are not both set (no stock objective sets both; the toolkit sets neither or Position).
- **Precedent for the new layout.** The mod SDK's manual and scripts, changed that day, say nothing of scenario
  packs, `.txn` or `.dbg`; none of the 11 subscribed Workshop mods had been rebuilt for 210942.
- **The gates, both ways.** `python -m modkit.ops.packs check` on the installed 201602 build of Twin Strike fails both:
  its Definition entries are `NDF/Scenarios/<Kind>/<scenario>.ndfbin`, and 39 of its classes and properties are in no
  210942 stock file (`TScenarioLoadInfo`, `TGDDescriptorLaunchEffetStandard`, `TClusterAddPythonPath`, the tags'
  `GUID1`..`GUID4`, `_ShortDatabaseName`, among them). Every stock Definition folder of the extraction passes both
  (the ops tests' stock sweeps), and the 210942 builds of Twin Strike, Open Door and the probes pass every gate.
