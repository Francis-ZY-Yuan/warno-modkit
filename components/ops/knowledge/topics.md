# Topics

The topics this component's mechanisms are filed under (see `knowledge/README.md` in the core component). Each
mechanism names one to three of them in its `topics` field.

## operation-setup: Operation setup
How an Operation is assembled from stock parts and registered: its packs, its identity, its deployment phase and its menu entry.

## script-flow: Script flow
How the script graph runs: containers, when each action finishes, loops, difficulty and how a battle ends.

## zones-and-map: Zones and map
The map side of an Operation: LevelDesign items and tags, zones and their owners, the playable zone, the AI grid and the camera.

## units-and-orders: Units and orders
Creating, revealing, ordering, counting and removing units, their effects, and the deck cards they come from.

## objectives-and-text: Objectives and text
Objectives, dialogs, counters, map labels and the text the player reads.
