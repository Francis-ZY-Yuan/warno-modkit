id: stock-deck-fits-its-own-mission
title: A stock deck is shaped for its own mission; check it holds what your op needs, where your op shows it
trials: [2026-11-11-probe3b-play1-no-supply]
---
RedJuggernaut's player deck has no supply helicopter, and its trucks sit only in its tank battalions' groups, so a
Soviet player on it found no supply. Before an op hands the player a stock deck, check that the player can buy what
the op needs (supply by road and by air, command units, transports) in the battlegroups the op shows, and add what is
missing at load. Twin Strike's stock test (`test_twin_strike_forces.StockSupply`) is that check for RedJuggernaut's
deck.
