**What we know.** The first probe (361 script objects) ran smoothly past 10:00 at its normal force size. The second
probe (518 objects) played 15 minutes without trouble. No play has gone past about 15 minutes, no script of ours has
reached stock's largest, and no limit on script size is known. Frame rate at a full mission's peak (well over 200
units) is unknown.
