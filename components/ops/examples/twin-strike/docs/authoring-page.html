Operation Twin Strike

Authoring page. This is the design page used to author the op with warno-modkit: a schematic of the plan, drawn from the op's own coordinates over the game's satellite picture of Twin Cities. It is not an in-game view.

Early July 1989, two weeks into the war. A Soviet tank division's forward detachment (you) must seize a road-junction village on the western edge of a pair of towns. NATO answers twice: a US armoured battalion counter-attacks at once, then a West German Panzer battalion assembles just south of the village for a heavier blow. Soviet doctrine of the day planned nuclear strikes against exactly that, a reserve massing to counter-attack, and Front command offers you release for one tactical nuclear strike. You may also refuse it and fight. One concentrated battle: the map is cut to the village and its fields, and everything happens there.

Player Soviet AI NATO, all scripted Map Twin Cities, the far-left village's corner Target 35-40 min Difficulty Easy, Medium, Hard
Drawn on the game's satellite picture of the op's cut of Twin Cities, north up: x 0-216, y 146-524 minimap pixels. The village's command zone is the stock one; the deployment boxes, circles, lanes and places are the op's own (twinstrike/map.py, twinstrike/op.py). Numbered badges give the phase.
0Deploy3 min

Buy and place the forward detachment in the north zone, with 3,000 points and no income. Two FOBs already stand there.

1Assault~10 min, deadline 20

Take the village from its pre-placed West German garrison, which holds it. It falls when the garrison is down to its breaking point and one of your command units is inside; the map flips to you.

Decidewhen to call the division's one preparation fire on the village (asked again every 2 min). Keep your own troops out. No fall by 20 min: the battle is lost.

2Unlock~2 min

Front releases the division's reserve into your deck, with 8,000 points at once and an income of 500 from then on. A hint reports NATO recon moving north.

3Counter-attack~12 min, cap 15

US battalion 1 comes from the south edge on two lanes: its recon screen first, then company teams with heavy air defence. Each wave attacks your units once it reaches the village.

  • NATO fighter sweeps: 2 F-15C over BOX W at +2:30, 2 over BOX C at +6:00, one more over the village at +10:00 on Hard.
  • It ends when battalion 1 is broken, or after 15 min with the village held; its survivors break off.

Decideeach fire mission: rockets or heavy guns, then BOX W or BOX C, on a cooldown. Once per battle, the division fire plan: every battery on whichever box holds more NATO vehicles.

4Surprise~5 min

A West German Panzer battalion appears at ASSEMBLY, about 5,200 GRU south of the village. It attacks in 05:00.

Deciderequest nuclear release, or fight it conventionally.

5Strike and hold5 min, or up to 20

Released: a 30 s warning, the camera flown to ASSEMBLY, and the game's own nuclear fireball on the battalion; part of it dies, the rest is left reeling for you to finish. Hold 5 min once it breaks.

Withheld: battalion 2 attacks north into the village, and 2 F-4F join once it attacks. Hold until it breaks or the village falls, up to 20 min. Both roads can reach the top grade.

Objectives and endings

Only the script ends the battle. Each objective label is pinned to its place on the map; B1, whose battalion comes on both lanes, shows in the panel only.

The end dialog names your choice: the strike released or withheld. There is no moral score; the choice changes the ending text and the battle before it.
