Operation Open Door

Authoring page. This is the design page used to author the op with warno-modkit: a schematic of the plan, drawn from the op's own coordinates over the game's minimap of Geisa. It is not an in-game view.

Three weeks into the war, NATO holds a salient across the border at Geisa. It knows a Soviet counter-stroke is coming to retake Fulda, and lets it in. A worn rearguard gives ground and draws the Soviets out onto open ground in front of an anvil far to the rear, and then you pick the moment to shut the door behind the forward detachment of the 5th Guards Tank Army's lead division, a reinforced T-80BV battalion.

Player NATO AI Soviet, all scripted Map stock Geisa, its own playable zone Target 45-60 min Shown the playable core: phases 1 to 4
Drawn on Geisa's own minimap, north up. Positions are its pixels (768 x 1024), from the op's own named points (design section 3, opendoor/map.py, opendoor/op.py); zones, circles and lines are the op's values. Numbered badges give the phase.
  1. 1The Screendeploy, then H to H+12

    Deploy from one deck: the worn 8th ID rearguard in the three PL BLUE towns, a blocking force on SHOULDER, and the anvil far back in the west.

    • H+1:00: the 39th GMRD's first echelon attacks the three towns, one company each.
    • Sell the retreat: bleed the first echelon, then give PL BLUE up. A town falls by capture or by a count race; its zone and its reinforcement entry then go Soviet.

    Decidehow long to hold. Still held at H+12: the main body comes anyway, and the objective fails with a dialog.

  2. 2The SackPL BLUE falls, to the spring

    The door is open: the forward detachment comes in six waves, one a minute, down the passage (MID) and the defile (BOTTOM), and out across the kill zone toward the anvil.

    • PL GREEN: the rearguard makes one more stand at the neck villages, then breaks contact for the anvil. When that window closes, both necks go Soviet.
    • The jaws stay hidden. Reinforcements now arrive only at the anvil.

    Decidewhen to spring the trap. Early: a small catch and a safe anvil. Late: a bigger catch, and the anvil may break.

  3. 3Close the Dooron your call

    The codeword. The north jaw, an M1A1HA company team, appears at RESERVE N and swings south through the central forest to NECK MID. The south jaw, a Leopard 2A4 company, drives out of HIDE S to NECK S.

    • Your deck gains M1A1HA, Leopard 2A4 and OH-58D cards, and the A-10A unlocks.
    • A neck is sealed when it holds your units and no Soviet unit for 30 s; its zone and its entry come back to you.
  4. 4The Cauldronto the end

    Both necks sealed: the Soviet vehicles in the pocket are counted, and it surrenders once a share of that count is left, or 15 min after the seal. What is left is removed and counted as captured.

    • The grade comes from the catch. If the pocket is not sealed 25 min after the spring, Corps ends the battle.

Objectives and endings

The aim is the catch: trap the forward detachment and destroy or capture as much of it as you can without losing the anvil. Primary objectives are the plan's steps and appear one phase at a time; only the anvil can lose the mission. The two bonuses are counted, but in the playable core they change nothing yet.

Grade and endings

The catch = main-body vehicles destroyed or captured, out of its 60. The script alone ends the battle.

Total victorycatch at least 70 %, and both necks sealed
Major victorycatch at least 40 %
Minor victorybelow that (the major screen, with its own dialog)
Backstopnot sealed 25 min after the spring: Corps ends it, and the catch counts only the destroyed
DefeatSoviets hold an anvil zone (4 units, none of yours, for 30 s); 6 main-body vehicles reach the rear exit; or your whole force is lost
