Mission design · _4x3_geisa · Fulda Gap, mid-July 1989

Operation Open Door

Three weeks into the war, NATO holds a salient across the border at Geisa. It knows a Soviet counter-stroke is coming to retake Fulda, and lets it in. NATO holds the north road firmly. In the centre and south, a worn rearguard gives ground and draws the Soviets out onto open ground in front of an anvil far to the rear, while a screen in the forests keeps Soviet recon away from the hidden reserves. Then you pick the moment to shut the door behind the forward detachment of the 5th Guards Tank Army's lead division, a reinforced T-80BV battalion racing for a deep objective. Pick a phase to see its moves. The objectives and the forces follow below the map. This is the full design (design.md); the example builds its playable core, phases 1 to 4 without the decoys, probes, strikes, air assault, Apaches and relief.

  1. 1The ScreenH to H+12

    The last company team of a worn 8th Infantry Division brigade holds the salient's forward edge (PL BLUE) against the rebuilt 39th GMRD, bleeds it, then gives the line up. The withdrawal toward the old border has to look like a spent force falling back. The north road is the exception: a shoulder dug in at its east mouth, with forest on both flanks, never yields.

    • Soviet opening barrage on PL BLUE at H-hour.
    • The rearguard falls back through the passage and the defile, makes one short stand at PL GREEN in the neck villages, then goes on to the anvil, far back near the west edge. Team A, in Town NE, falls back into the shoulder.
    • A counter-recon screen holds each forest track: cavalry scouts on the trip-wire and a reinforced infantry platoon with an anti-tank team. 3rd AD mech infantry hold the north tracks; two platoons of the Leopard company's Panzergrenadiere guard their hide in the south.
    • Make it look real: kill first-echelon units and let the Soviets burn the 8 decoys (8th ID M1s with broken tracks and dead guns, left in the fighting positions) before you yield. The better the lie, the more of the main body walks in.
  2. 2The SackH+12 to ~H+28

    The forward detachment of the 5th Guards Tank Army's lead division, a reinforced T-80BV battalion, pours down the two open lanes, the passage (mid) and the defile, and out onto open ground, where the anvil kills them at long range. A fixing attack hits the shoulder, which has to hold. The M1A1HA company team waits behind it on the north road, the Leopards wait in HIDE S, and the hammer's cards stay locked.

    • First, Teams B and C make a 2-minute stand at PL GREEN in the neck villages, then break contact for the anvil. A beaten force that fights twice is easier to believe.
    • Recon probes push down the forest tracks toward the reserves. The screen has to kill them, or slow them until you spring the trap.
    • A probe that reaches a reserve exposes it: a Soviet force blocks that jaw's path, and a tank company stays out of the sack. Holding PL BLUE past H+12 exposes HIDE S the same way.
    • No Soviet shelling: the guns are moving up behind an advance that thinks NATO is beaten.
    • Corps asks: spring the trap now, or wait for a bigger catch?
  3. 3Close the Dooron your call

    The 3rd AD's own UH-60As land its air cav troopers, with an Apache pair overhead, just north of the passage village and cut the middle lane (NECK MID). The M1A1HA company team swings south from behind the shoulder, through the central forest, to link up with them. The reinforced Leopard 2A4 company drives out of the southern forest to the defile (NECK S).

    • One lift, flown in over NATO-held forest.
    • Higher-echelon bomb strikes on a few named boxes: the approaches east of both necks, plus interdiction at the Town NE junction to delay the next echelon.
  4. 4The Cauldronto the end

    The pocket tries to break out east through the necks while a T-80U relief force hits them from outside. Hold both necks and grind the pocket down until it surrenders.

    • The relief opens its attack with one barrage on the necks, announced before impact.
    • NATO's assault on the pocket opens with a J-SEAD strike on its air defence.
    • F-4G Wild Weasel cards unlock to finish the radars. Then an AH-64A company comes in over the anvil to kill the trapped tanks, plus a second company if the pocket is blind.
    • Grade = share of the main body destroyed or captured, against what escaped.

Objectives

The aim is the catch: trap the lead division's forward detachment and destroy or capture as much of them as you can without losing the anvil. Primary objectives are the plan's steps, shown one phase at a time. Only the anvil can lose the mission; any other failed step costs part of the catch, and a dialog says so. Every bonus changes the fight that follows, not just the score.

PrimaryLet them in"Soviet vehicles in the sack: n / S_min." At S_min, Corps asks whether to spring the trap. Not answered by H+30: Corps springs it anyway.
BonusKeep the reserves hiddenNo reserve exposed before the spring. Done: both jaws go in with no Soviet force blocking them. Exposed: a blocking force at that neck, and a tank company stays out of the sack.
BonusMake a stand at PL GREENHold a neck village for 2:00 once the Soviets reach it, then break contact. +1 credibility: a company waiting at Town NE comes in after all.

The anvil and the shoulder objectives carry on.

3Close the Door

PrimaryShut the doorHold NECK MID and NECK S: your units in each, no Soviets, for a short while. Until both are sealed: Soviets that slip out count as escaped.

The air-assault teams only have to hold NECK MID until the M1A1HA arrive.

PrimaryReduce the pocketIt surrenders once its vehicles fall to about 35 % of what was sealed in, with both necks held. A long backstop (out of fuel and ammunition) stops it stalling.
Primary · to the endKeep the door shutHold both necks against the relief. A neck falls: the pocket reopens until you retake it.
BonusBlind the pocketIts air defence down to one vehicle or none. Done: the Apaches come at once, and a second company follows.
BonusBreak the reliefDestroy 16 relief tanks. Done: the relief's last wave is called off.

Credibility: how much walks in

Three bonuses score a point each: the two in Phase 1 and the stand at PL GREEN. Any two bring the whole main body in. The score is read when the main body is released and again when the stand ends, and a dialog says what the Soviets made of it.

ScoreSoviet readingWhat happens
3"NATO is routed"The whole main body comes in, and one recon probe fewer goes into the forests.
2"NATO is broken"The whole main body comes into the sack.
1"Probably broken"One of the three T-80BV companies waits at Town NE, then joins the relief against the necks.
0"Too easy"Two companies wait, and an extra recon probe goes into the forests.

Grade

Catch = main-body vehicles destroyed or captured ÷ main-body vehicles in play. Companies held back by low credibility or an exposed reserve still count in that total, so the lie and the screen both raise the ceiling.

GradeNeeds
Total victoryCatch ≥ 70 %, with the anvil, the shoulder and both necks held
Major victoryCatch ≥ 40 %
Minor victoryThe anvil held

Targets scale with difficulty: Make them pay 10 / 15 / 20 kills, decoys 3 / 4 / 5, relief tanks 11 / 16 / 20, surrender at 45 / 35 / 25 %.
