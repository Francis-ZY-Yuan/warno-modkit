title: After a failed launch, find the root cause, gate it, fix, reinstall hash-checked, and update the play sheet
trials: [2026-11-03-probe1-launch1-damagetype, 2026-11-03-probe1-launch2-dialog-buttons, 2026-11-03-probe1-launch3-no-arrivals]
---
A launch costs the player minutes and a gate costs seconds. Let the player run the game, and spend the effort on
checks before a build is handed over. Reinstall the fixed build, check its hashes against the build output, and
update the play sheet so the player can just start the game.
