title: Design probe results to show on the map, and debrief with a few multiple-choice questions about what was seen

A player who plays for fun misses about a third of the readout rows but remembers zone colours, units vanishing,
routes and the end screen. Put the decisive unknowns first, ask about what was visible, always offer "didn't watch",
and make test cards hard to miss: plain-looking test cards were never bought.
