WARNO build 212400 stopped every build at one gate, as each update does: `ndfrules.check_exe` found an installed
WARNO.exe other than the one the rules table was read from. `setup extract` ran on the declarations as they are, and
`ndfrules extract` read the new exe. No code change was needed beyond the packaging stamp.

Twin Strike's Workshop copy, built on 211352, was active on 212400 but stopped at load for every player: "NDF parsing
error. Retry?" and "File not found" for its own `Scenario.ndf`. Its compiled transactions' tags held 211352's values:
the source hashes of `ScenarioInfos.ndf`, `ScenarioLoader.ndf`, `ScenarioGameplayConstants.ndf` and
`UICommonEnum.ndf`, and the file lists of the MapConfiguration scan and the ScenarioLoader transaction from before
they lost `Globals.ndf`. The game took those transactions for stale and compiled them from sources the mod does not
ship ([[txn-tag-config-hash]]). Rebuilt on the 212400 extraction, Twin Strike's and Open Door's tags hold stock
212400's source hashes, and each transaction's file list is stock Red Juggernaut's apart from the scenario's name.

What changed for the stock corpus. The chain grew by a sixth revision, as 211352's did: a `211352/212400/` delta
folder (1,356 archives, two of them holding files: 27 NDF sources, the scenario templates among them) and a top-level
`212400/` folder with a new `Transactions.dat` and a full copy of every map, scenario and decor set `_Definition`
pack. 9,483 archives with 162,309 entries, all read. In every stock scenario, `ScenarioInfo.txn` and
`ScenarioLoader.txn` changed (with their `.dbg`), and so did every tag: the ScenarioLoader transaction lost
`Globals.ndf` and gained `Clusters/ReloadableType.ndf`, the MapConfiguration and TrophyDef scans lost `Globals.ndf`,
and the tags hold new hashes for `ScenarioInfos.ndf`, `ScenarioLoader.ndf`, `ScenarioGameplayConstants.ndf` and
`UICommonEnum.ndf`. `GDScript.txn` and `MapConfiguration.txn` did not change. Thirty-two of the game's own
transactions changed too. No declared file or folder is missing.

What changed in the load-time rules: nothing. The table holds the same 3,663 rules on 1,415 classes, row for row; only
the stock counts moved, on 10 classes. Each stock `ScenarioLoader.txn` holds one cluster load fewer, and
`IA/Cluster.txn` one cluster generation fewer. `DepictionCore.txn` lost six of its twelve chassis descriptors, and
`Scenario/GFX/Constantes.txn` now also holds the strategic AI constants. All 663 stock files of 212400 (1.56 million
objects) pass.

The port moves packaging to 212400 in `Gen/Version.ndf` and `ModGenVersion`, by the pairing rule. Twin Strike's
Workshop copy ships `Gen/Version.ndf` 211352, while its upload on 212400 rewrote `ModGenVersion` to 212400
([[2026-11-18-twin-strike-workshop-update]]). It was active on 212400, so the stamp is not what broke it
([[mod-version-and-config-lines]]).
