# Maintainers' notes

How this project handles requests and notices. Scope first: warno-modkit is for single-player scenario mods only.

- **Issues.** Requests for multiplayer advantages, or for changing or getting around the game executable, are closed
  unanswered.
- **A GitHub DMCA notice.** Comply narrowly, inside GitHub's one-business-day window: edit or remove the named files
  and nothing more.
- **An email from Eugen Systems.** Answer politely and remove what it names.
- **Counter-notices.** Only for a genuine mistake, and only after legal advice. A counter-notice gives the sender's name, address and phone number to the complainant.
  A circumvention (§1201) claim gets GitHub's own technical and legal review.
- **Scope.** Single-player scenario mods only.
